01 — THE IDEA

Small hardware.
Useful intelligence.

Manual counting can interrupt a class and invite mistakes. This prototype automates the count by detecting the order in which two IR sensors are triggered.

⌘
THE CHALLENGE

Know how many
people are inside.

Classroom occupancy is easy to lose track of when people move in and out throughout the day.

Manual tracking↗
✓
OUR APPROACH

Automatic, directional counting.

Two IR sensors identify the direction of movement. The Arduino updates the count and sends it to the LCD over I²C.

Embedded system↗
